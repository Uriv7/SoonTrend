What is SoonTrend?

SoonTrend is an independent editorial platform focused on one thing: taking the topics the world is actively searching for and explaining them with depth, clarity, and accuracy. We publish new articles three times every day — morning, afternoon, and evening.

We believe that when something is trending, people deserve more than a headline. They deserve context, history, expert perspective, and clear answers to the questions they're actually asking.

How SoonTrend Works

1

We track what the world is searching

Our system monitors trending topics from news sources, social platforms, and search trends across 30+ countries — updated three times every day.

2

Our editorial AI drafts the article

We use state-of-the-art AI language models to produce a structured first draft — covering background, key facts, expert context, and common questions.

3

AI-Assisted Editorial Disclosure

SoonTrend uses artificial intelligence (Google Gemini and Groq's Llama models) to help research and draft articles at speed. All content is generated with strict editorial guidelines — no geographic bias, no sensationalism, no misinformation. We disclose this openly because transparency builds trust.

AI-generated content has limitations. While we strive for accuracy, readers should verify critical information from primary sources before making important decisions.
