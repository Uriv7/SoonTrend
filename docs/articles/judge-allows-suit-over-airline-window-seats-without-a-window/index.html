However, in recent years, some airlines have begun to blur the lines between window and non-window seats. This has led to a growing number of complaints from passengers who feel they have been misled by airlines regarding the features of their seats.

A study conducted by the Consumer Reports National Research Center found that 71% of passengers believe that window seats should come with a window. However, this is not always the case. In fact, some airlines have begun to charge extra for seats with windows, while others have simply rebranded non-window seats as 'window seats' without any real change in amenities.
