How Does a Mental Competency Exam Work in the Justice System?

A mental competency exam is typically conducted by a psychiatrist or psychologist, who will assess the defendant's mental state and determine their ability to understand the charges against them. This process can involve a comprehensive evaluation of the defendant's mental health history, cognitive function, and behavioral patterns. The examiner will also review any relevant medical records, testify to the defendant's ability to understand the charges, and provide expert testimony in court. The results of the exam can be used to determine whether the defendant is competent to stand trial, and if not, whether they can be restored to competency through treatment or counseling. In some cases, the defendant may be found to be incompetent to stand trial, in which case the court may order them to undergo treatment or counseling.
