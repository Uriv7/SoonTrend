A Citizen Vigilante is an individual who takes the law into their own hands, often in response to a perceived failure of the justice system. This can manifest in various forms, including self-organized vigilantism, community policing, and grassroots justice initiatives. The rise of the Citizen Vigilante phenomenon is a reflection of the growing distrust in institutions and the desire for individuals to take control of their safety and well-being. According to a study published in the Journal of Crime and Delinquency, the number of vigilante groups has increased by 30% over the past decade, highlighting the need for a deeper understanding of this complex issue.
