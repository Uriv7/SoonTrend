What is Nuclear Fusion?

Nuclear Fusion is a process in which two or more atomic nuclei combine to form a single, heavier nucleus, releasing vast amounts of energy in the process. This is the same process that powers the sun and other stars, and it's a reaction that has been occurring naturally for billions of years. The potential of Nuclear Fusion is immense, and it's an area of research that has been gaining momentum in recent years. Scientists and engineers are working to develop the technology needed to harness this energy, and several approaches are being explored, including magnetic confinement and inertial confinement. By creating a device that can contain and control the fusion reaction, it may be possible to generate electricity at a fraction of the cost of traditional power plants, while also reducing greenhouse gas emissions to near zero. Nuclear Fusion is a game-changer, and it's an idea that has the potential to transform the way we live and work.
