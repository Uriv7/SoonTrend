How Does Cape Verde Work: A Guide to the Islands' Unique Culture

Cape Verde is a unique blend of African, Portuguese, and Brazilian cultures, making it a fascinating destination for travelers and adventurers. The Cape Verdean people are known for their warm hospitality, love of music and dance, and rich cultural heritage. The islands' unique culture is reflected in their cuisine, which is a blend of African, Portuguese, and Brazilian flavors. Popular dishes include cachupa, a hearty stew made with corn, beans, and meat, and frango grelhado, a grilled chicken dish served with rice and vegetables.
