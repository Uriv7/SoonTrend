What Makes a Wedding Dress Truly Haute Couture?

A wedding dress is considered haute couture when it meets the strict criteria set by the Chambre Syndicale de la Haute Couture, which includes being handmade, requiring at least 20 hours of labor, and being created by a member of the Chambre Syndicale.
