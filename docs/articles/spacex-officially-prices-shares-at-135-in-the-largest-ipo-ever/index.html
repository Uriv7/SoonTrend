How Does SpaceX's Record-Breaking IPO Work?

The process of an IPO is complex and involves several stages. First, the company must file a registration statement with the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), which outlines the terms of the offering. This statement must include information about the company's financial performance, management team, and business strategy. Once the registration statement has been filed, the company can begin to market the offering to potential investors. This is typically done through a roadshow, where the company's management team presents its case to investors and answers their questions. The roadshow is followed by a period of marketing, where the company's investment bankers promote the offering to their clients. Once the marketing period has ended, the company can begin to price the offering. This is typically done through a series of auctions, where investors bid on the shares. The price of the shares is determined by the highest bid, and the company can then use this money to fund its growth and development.
