What Is Chronic Pelvic Pain? (Definition and Overview)

Chronic pelvic pain is a persistent pain in the pelvic area that lasts for more than 3 months. It affects millions of people worldwide, causing significant distress and disrupting daily life. According to a study published in the Journal of Pain Research, 1 in 5 adults experience chronic pelvic pain. The causes of chronic pelvic pain are complex and multifaceted, involving a combination of physical, emotional, and psychological factors. Physical causes include musculoskeletal issues, such as pelvic floor dysfunction, and gastrointestinal problems, like irritable bowel syndrome. Emotional and psychological factors, such as anxiety, depression, and stress, can also contribute to chronic pelvic pain. A study published in the Journal of Clinical Rheumatology found that 75% of patients with chronic pelvic pain also experienced anxiety or depression.
