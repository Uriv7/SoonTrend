In a landmark decision, a federal judge has ruled against immigration arrests at US courthouses, citing concerns over public safety and the potential for chilling effects on immigrant communities. The ruling, which applies nationwide, blocks a policy implemented by the US government that allowed Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) agents to make arrests inside courthouses. This move has been met with widespread criticism from immigrant rights groups and lawmakers, who argue that it undermines the integrity of the justice system and puts vulnerable individuals at risk. The ruling is seen as a major victory for immigrant advocates, who have long argued that courthouses should be safe spaces for those seeking justice, regardless of their immigration status.
